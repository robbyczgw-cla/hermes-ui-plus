Hermes Interactive UI+

Interactive chat for Hermes Agent. Approve commands from your phone, watch every tool step live, leave the app while Hermes keeps working, and get answers that are not walls of text.

Get the Android alpha Self-hosting View source on GitHub

Made for people who run Hermes Agent and talk to it through Telegram or the terminal today. Requires Hermes Agent v0.21 or newer and a small bridge that you run yourself.

What it does

  • Approvals

    When Hermes asks before running a command, you see the command and answer in the app.

  • Live tool steps

    A timeline shows what ran and how long it took, while the answer streams in.

  • Leave the app

    Hermes keeps working in the background; the finished answer waits for you.

  • Interactive answers

    About 50 components: tables, charts, checklists, maps, recipes, quizzes, timers. Your taps go back to Hermes as the next message.

  • Local-first

    Conversations stay on your device and your own server. No accounts, no telemetry.

  • Your server only

    The app talks to your bridge, never to a service of ours. The bridge holds the Hermes API key.

Install and self-hosting

There is no hosted service. You run a small Node.js service, the bridge, next to your Hermes (Node.js 22+ or Docker). It authenticates your devices, routes presentation, handles uploads, voice and push, and pairs a new phone with a QR code. We strongly recommend reaching it over Tailscale and never exposing it to the public internet.

  1. Turn on the Hermes API server and start the Hermes gateway.
  2. Run the setup wizard on that machine: npx hermes-ui-plus-bridge setup.
  3. Install the app and scan the pairing QR code.

Privacy in short

The developers collect no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your messages go from your device to your bridge, then to your Hermes, and from there to the model providers you configured. Nowhere else. Read the full privacy policy.
